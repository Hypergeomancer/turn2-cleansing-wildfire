🔥 Jund Wildfire Calculator

Probability of the Turn-2 Wildfire-on-Bridge Sequence in Pauper

Purpose:

Compute the probability of executing the Jund Wildfire turn-two sequence (bridge on T1, Cleansing Wildfire on the bridge on T2) for a given deck composition.

How to use:

Enter the number of bridges, non-bridge lands, and copies of Cleansing Wildfire in the deck. The calculator returns the probability for three observation windows — opening hand, on the play, and on the draw.

1. Deck Composition

Bridges enter tapped (so they cannot be the untapped T2 land); non-bridge lands are assumed to enter untapped. Other cards include everything else in the deck.
